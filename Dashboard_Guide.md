# Power BI Dashboard Guide

The dashboard translates the enterprise credit assessment into a five-page monitoring interface.

| Page | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 01 — Executive Credit Overview | Executive view of revenue, EBITDA, interest coverage, leverage and the overall lending position |
| 02 — Financial Performance & Cash Generation | Earnings quality, operating cash generation and cash-conversion trends |
| 03 — Debt, Leverage & Repayment Capacity | Borrowings, leverage, debt structure, maturity/refinancing considerations and repayment capacity |
| 04 — Liquidity, FX & Contingent Risk | Current liquidity, working capital, FX exposure and contingent liabilities |
| 05 — Early Warning & Credit Monitoring | Monitoring indicators and early-warning signals for post-approval oversight |

The dashboard is intended to support a credit reviewer or Credit Committee rather than function as a general-purpose business intelligence dashboard.
