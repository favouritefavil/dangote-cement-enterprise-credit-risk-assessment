## Evidence treatment

The project distinguishes between direct evidence, figures that can be derived/extracted from disclosed information, information not disclosed in the reviewed reports, and information requiring an external source. No missing value was silently converted to zero.

## External-source requirements

For a live lending engagement, external verification may include CBN/NBS macroeconomic data, NGX/SEC filings, credit-rating information and facility-specific documentation. These were outside the public-data scope of the portfolio case study unless explicitly incorporated in the final assessment.

## Note on source files

The repository contains the project's evidence review and analytical artifacts rather than reproducing the full underlying annual-report PDFs. The annual reports remain the primary source documents underlying the evidence review.
