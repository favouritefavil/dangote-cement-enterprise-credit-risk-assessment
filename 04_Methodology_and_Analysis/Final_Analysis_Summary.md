# Final Analysis Summary

## Current credit position

FY2025 represents a materially stronger credit position than FY2024. Revenue increased to **₦4,306.7bn**, EBITDA to **₦1,981.3bn**, operating profit to **₦1,765.3bn**, and operating cash flow to **₦1,710.8bn**. Gross borrowings declined to **₦1,080.5bn** from approximately ₦2,511.8bn in FY2024.

## Debt service and leverage

Interest coverage improved from **1.65x in FY2024 to 5.10x in FY2025**. Debt/EBITDA declined from approximately **1.82x to 0.55x**, while net debt/EBITDA was approximately **0.34x**.

## Liquidity

FY2025 current assets were approximately **₦1,972.0bn** against current liabilities of **₦2,603.7bn**, giving a **0.76x current ratio**. Short-term debt of approximately **₦799.8bn** remains an important refinancing-monitoring item.

## Cash generation

Operating cash flow of **₦1,710.8bn** exceeded EBITDA and remained the principal source of debt-service capacity. After approximately ₦861.1bn of capital expenditure, cash after capex was approximately ₦849.7bn before other financing requirements.

## FX and contingent liabilities

USD-denominated liabilities declined sharply from the FY2024 peak of approximately ₦1,954.7bn to **₦516.1bn in FY2025**, but residual FX exposure remains material. Group contingent liabilities increased from approximately **₦133.5bn in FY2022 to ₦457.4bn in FY2025**, requiring continued monitoring.

## Related-party dependency

The FY2025 deleveraging benefited from a significant related-party repayment. The assessment therefore distinguishes **core operating repayment capacity** from non-recurring related-party support and does not assume that such support will recur.

## Final credit view

**POSITIVE / STRONG CREDIT PROFILE, SUBJECT TO STRUCTURE AND MONITORING.**

**Preliminary lending recommendation: LEND — subject to facility-specific due diligence, appropriate structuring, risk appetite/limit approval and ongoing monitoring.**

This is a portfolio case study and not an actual facility approval or lending commitment.
