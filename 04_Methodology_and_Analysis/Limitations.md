# Assumptions, Limitations & Due-Diligence Requirements

## Public-data limitations

The assessment is based primarily on publicly available audited annual reports for FY2022–FY2025 and the validated project dataset. It does not include private management information, an actual facility request, private facility agreements, lender-specific security valuations or confidential covenant schedules.

## Key limitations

- Existing financial covenant terms and ratios were not fully disclosed in the reviewed public materials.
- Quantified contracted forward capital commitments were not located as a discrete disclosure in the source review.
- Detailed hedging arrangements require further verification where relevant to facility structuring.
- Customer/supplier concentration information is limited in public disclosures.
- Dangote Industries Limited's standalone financial condition is outside the scope of Dangote Cement Plc's own annual reports.
- Public disclosures do not provide sufficient information to independently value individual collateral/security assets.
- FY2022 interest coverage is not presented because a validated comparable interest-only figure was unavailable.
- Certain cross-year subsidiary composition, ownership and IAS 29 scope changes require care when interpreting long-run comparability.

## Facility-specific due diligence

Before an actual lending decision, a lender would ordinarily obtain and verify items such as:

- facility request and purpose;
- existing debt schedule and contractual maturities;
- covenant package and headroom;
- security package and independent collateral values;
- management accounts and cash-flow forecasts;
- detailed FX and interest-rate exposure;
- material litigation and contingent-liability status;
- related-party funding/support arrangements; and
- updated management and lender information.

## Interpretation discipline

The final credit view is based on the evidence and assumptions documented in the project. Stress scenarios are illustrative sensitivities, not forecasts. Borrower management statements about contingent liabilities or expected outcomes are treated as management representations rather than independently verified conclusions.
