# Credit Assessment Methodology

## Purpose

This project simulates a corporate-bank enterprise credit assessment of Dangote Cement Plc using FY2022–FY2025 public financial information. The objective is to determine whether the borrower demonstrates sustainable repayment capacity and to identify the risks that could weaken that capacity.

## Assessment basis

- **Borrower:** Dangote Cement Plc
- **Period:** FY2022–FY2025
- **Primary assessment year:** FY2025
- **Reporting basis:** Group / consolidated
- **Primary evidence:** audited annual reports and related disclosures for FY2022–FY2025
- **Analytical support:** validated credit-analysis workbook and Power BI monitoring dashboard

## Analytical sequence

1. Establish the borrower, group structure and reporting basis.
2. Assess business profile, operating footprint and structural risks.
3. Analyse revenue, profitability and EBITDA performance.
4. Assess operating cash generation and cash conversion.
5. Evaluate debt structure, leverage and repayment capacity.
6. Evaluate liquidity and working-capital requirements.
7. Assess FX and interest-rate exposure.
8. Review contingent liabilities, parent guarantees and related-party exposure.
9. Apply illustrative downside sensitivities to test resilience.
10. Identify key risks and mitigating factors.
11. Translate the analysis into a preliminary lending view and monitoring framework.

## Core credit principles used

### Source of repayment
The primary source of repayment is operating cash flow generated by the core cement business. Reported profitability is therefore assessed alongside cash generation rather than treated as sufficient evidence of repayment capacity on its own.

### Leverage
Gross debt is considered alongside EBITDA, while net leverage recognises the offset provided by cash and cash equivalents.

### Debt service
Interest coverage is assessed using operating profit relative to the validated interest-expense basis used in the signed-off analysis. FY2022 interest coverage is not presented because a comparable validated interest-only figure was not available.

### Liquidity
Current assets, current liabilities, cash and short-term debt are considered together. A sub-1.0x current ratio is treated as a monitoring signal rather than an automatic distress conclusion because refinancing capacity and operating cash generation also matter.

### Risk transmission
Business, cost, FX, interest-rate, working-capital and contingent-liability risks are assessed through their potential effect on margins, operating cash flow, debt service and refinancing flexibility.

### Stress testing
The stress tests are illustrative sensitivities rather than forecasts or probability-weighted scenarios. They test individual and combined downside effects on revenue, EBITDA, interest burden and operating cash flow.

## Credit decision discipline

The final lending view is conditional rather than an unconditional facility approval. Facility-specific due diligence, structuring, risk appetite/limit approval, security considerations, covenants and ongoing monitoring remain necessary before an actual lending decision.

## Data discipline

Where public information was unavailable, the project flags the limitation rather than inventing a value. Where a figure is derived from disclosed information, the underlying source and treatment are documented in the dataset and source evidence review.
